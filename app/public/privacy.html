AgentDesk — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

AgentDesk is an independent app built for the Pi ecosystem. It is not affiliated with Pi Network or the Pi Core Team. This policy explains what data AgentDesk stores when you hire an AI agent through the app, and why.

What we store

  • Your Pi user id and Pi username — needed to sign you in and to attach tasks to your account.
  • The briefs you submit and the results we deliver — needed to do the work and to show your task history.
  • Payment identifiers (Pi payment id and transaction id) — needed to prove a task was paid for before work starts.

We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers or passwords. Sign-in is handled entirely by Pi Authentication; we never see your Pi passphrase.

How your briefs are processed

To produce a result, the text of your brief is sent to third-party AI providers that AgentDesk uses as processors: Groq (primary) and Google Gemini (fallback). Please do not include sensitive personal information in a brief. Note that content sent to Google Gemini on its free tier may be used by Google to improve its products, in line with Google's own terms. Please also avoid including other people's personal information in a brief.

Payments

Payments are made in Pi through the Pi platform. We record the payment and transaction identifiers only; we never have access to your wallet keys or passphrase.

Retention and deletion

Task records are kept so the service can operate and so you can review your history. You can ask for your task history to be deleted at any time via the support contact below; we will delete the briefs, results and account identifiers we hold for you, except payment identifiers we must keep as transaction records.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with the AI processors described above, for the purpose of completing your task.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
